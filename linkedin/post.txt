Built a simple Feedback Board for my Module 3 exercise on Cloud Services & Web App Deployment.

The app uses Python/FastAPI and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Visitors can submit a short message and view recent feedback. I tested the local save-and-refresh flow and prepared a free Render + Supabase PostgreSQL deployment, with server-side environment variables, a health endpoint, and basic request and storage limits.

GitHub: https://github.com/Gauravwakle96/module-3

The attached screenshots show the local preview. Cloud deployment is the next step.
