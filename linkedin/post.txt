Built a simple Feedback Board for my Module 3 exercise on Cloud Services & Web App Deployment.

The app uses Python/FastAPI and HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Visitors can submit a short message and view recent feedback. I deployed it using Render Free and Supabase Free PostgreSQL, with server-side environment variables, a health endpoint, and basic request and storage limits.

Live website: https://module3-feedback-board.onrender.com

GitHub: https://github.com/Gauravwakle96/module-3

The attached screenshots show the live website. I verified that feedback remains saved after refreshing the page.
